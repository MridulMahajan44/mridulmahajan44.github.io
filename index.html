Mridul Mahajan

Hi, I'm Mridul.

I'm a third-year PhD student at Boston University, where I'm advised by Aldo Pacchiano. I'm broadly interested in reinforcement learning from an algorithmic perspective, with a focus on how transfer, structure, and diversity can improve upon tabula-rasa learning in terms of sample efficiency and performance.

research —Reinforcement Learning · Representation Learning · Post-Training · Reasoning

Previously, I was a guest researcher in the Empirical Inference department at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS), and a visiting scholar in the Machine Teaching Group at the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems (MPI-SWS).
